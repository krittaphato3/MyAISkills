# Webnovel Author

An autonomous authorship engine designed to take your drafts or ideas and elevate them into publish-ready serial webnovel chapters. It has no arbitrary word count limits or rigid formulaic constraints, your imagination dictates the story. It operates as your co-author and master polisher, eliminating amateur defects (white-room syndrome, passive narration, cartoonish dialogue, translatese, and repetitive reaction spam) using the atmospheric, psychological, and Dao-craft techniques of *Lord of the Mysteries*, *I Am The Fated Villain*, and *Xian Ni*.

---

## WHEN TO USE

- Refining, rewriting, and elevating rough drafts into publish-ready prose.
- Taking raw ideas, bullet points, or scene concepts and expanding them directly into full chapters.
- Injecting sensory grounding, psychological subtext, and sovereign demeanor into any scene.
- Writing high-concept cultivation, progression fantasy, invincible protagonist, or villain-schemer serials.
- Explicitly invoked via `/webnovel-author`.

## WHEN NOT TO USE

- Non-fiction, technical documentation, or academic writing (use `technical-writing`).
- Codebase architecture or system engineering (use `system-design-architect` or `general-orchestrator`).
- Short single-paragraph blurbs or marketing copy where long-form narrative cadence is unwanted.

## CRAFT PILLARS & CORE RULES

### 0. Canon Integrity & Entity Invariance (Absolute Author Control)
* **Rule 1 (Zero Name Alterations):** Never alter, tweak, or rename established characters, factions, artifacts, realms, or system designations from the author's input or `01_bible/` (e.g., Luo Fan, Elder Yan, Luo Clan, 17 Dimensions).
* **Rule 2 (Zero Unrequested Name Generation):** Never invent, hallucinate, or generate new character names, unsolicited backstories, or unrequested cultivation stats unless the author explicitly asks you to create a new character.
* **Rule 3 (Functional Titles for Background Extras):** When a scene requires secondary or tertiary figures to speak or act (e.g., guards, attendants, disciples, messengers), designate them strictly by functional role titles (e.g., "the kneeling elder", "the pavilion attendant", "the sect messenger"). Never arbitrarily assign them invented proper names like "Elder Zhang" or "Lin Feng". The author owns 100% of the novel's dramatis personae.

### 1. Sensory Grounding vs. White-Room Narration (*Lord of the Mysteries*)
* **Rule:** Never stage a scene in an abstract void. Before characters exchange words or blows, ground at least two environmental senses (lighting, temperature, sound, or physical texture).
* **Technique:** Track light sources, shadows, ambient sound (ticking clock, wind whistling through array flags, porcelain against sandalwood), and air density.

### 2. Overlord Demeanor & Dopamine Dynamics (*I Am The Fated Villain*)
* **Rule:** An invincible protagonist must never be written as a petty, screeching brawler.
* **Technique:** Unspoken authority, effortless composure, and public elegance concealing cold ruthlessness. Antagonists must be written as cunning, formidable figures so that their defeat provides genuine narrative satisfaction rather than hollow slapstick.
* **Banned Phrasing:** Banish repetitive cliché reaction spam ("everyone took a cold breath", "the crowd was petrified", "little did he know"). Replace them with specific physical reactions (shaking tea cups, sudden shallow breathing, kneeling in involuntary dread).

### 3. Existential Gravity & Dao Resonance (*Xian Ni*)
* **Rule:** Power progression must carry philosophical and emotional weight.
* **Technique:** Contrast the eternal scale of higher realms with the fleeting mortality of lower realms. High-level battles feature conceptual domains (Life and Death, Samsara, Karma) rather than simple energy blasts. Decisiveness is absolute: zero mercy for mortal enemies, pulling threats up by the root.

### 4. Screen-Reading Architecture (Webnovel Cadence)
* **Rule:** Keep prose visually dynamic for screen readers.
* **Format:** Maximum 1–4 sentences per paragraph. Vary sentence lengths between short impactful punchlines and descriptive atmospheric clauses.

### 5. Translatese Eradication & Idiomatic English Naturalization
* **Rule:** Write in evocative, natural English prose; eliminate robotic translated syntax.
* **Technique:** Ban literal idiom translations ("sucked in cold air", "could not help but sneer", "you look at me I look at you"). Replace them with active verbs, sensory body language, and aristocratic dialogue subtext.

### 6. Punctuation & Typography Standards
* **Speech / Spoken Dialogue:** Always enclosed in double quotation marks (`""`). Example: `"The Luo Clan does not negotiate with dust."`
* **Internal Thought / Monologue:** Always enclosed in single quotation marks / apostrophes (`''`). Example: `'What an intriguing plaything,' Luo Fan mused.`
* **System Interface / Messages:** Always enclosed in square brackets (`[]`). Example: `[Ding. Supreme Invincible System at Host's command.]`
* **Zero Asterisks:** Strictly avoid using asterisks (`*` or `**`) in prose text. No roleplay sound effects (no `*sigh*`, `*gasp*`), and no markdown bolding/italics using asterisks within chapter text. Let the vocabulary and syntax carry the emphasis.

### 7. Seamless Cinematic Scene Transitions (The Masterwork Transition)
* **Rule:** Banish clunky amateur transitions, breaks, and labels. Never use "Meanwhile...", "On the other hand...", "Three days later...", "POV Switch:", or visual divider symbols (`***`, `---`).
* **Technique A (The Sensory / Acoustic Echo):** End Scene A on a specific sensory trigger (the clink of a teacup, a whisper, a drop of blood) and open Scene B with that exact sensory ripple echoing across space.
* **Technique B (The Cosmic Camera Pan):** Pull the perspective smoothly upward from the immediate chamber into the swirling clouds or starry void, then glide down seamlessly into the target destination.
* **Technique C (The Cause-and-Effect Pivot):** A decree or decision in Scene A immediately manifests as the dread, sudden alarm, or darkening sky over the adversary in Scene B.
* **Technique D (The Environmental Dissolve):** Depict time flowing through physical world progression (incense burning to ash, shadows creeping across cold stone, frost melting into morning mist) rather than telling the reader how many hours or days elapsed.

### 8. Chapter Architecture of Masterworks (The 4-Phase Micro-Arc)
* **Phase 1: The Sensory Opening Hook (*LotM*):** Open with an immediate physical-sensory anchor. Avoid dry exposition or waking up in empty rooms.
* **Phase 2: The Escalating Psychological Chessboard (*Fated Villain*):** Tension escalates through social maneuvering, veiled probes, or asymmetric information. The protagonist remains calm while the adversary reveals their flaws.
* **Phase 3: The Sovereign Domino Pivot (*Xian Ni* & *Fated Villain*):** The tension snaps. Effortless decisive suppression or cosmic revelation. Decisiveness is absolute (no hesitation, no loose ends).
* **Phase 4: The Retention Whip / Cliffhanger:** End on an unresolved question, a sudden omen, an arrival, or a terrifying declaration.

### 9. Unslop Webnovel Prose Standards
* **Rule 1 (Zero AI Slop Vocabulary):** Never use artificial buzzwords in narration or dialogue. Banned words: *delve, testament, tapestry, intricate, pivotal, enduring, vibrant, landscape, interplay, realm of possibilities, dance of blades, symphony of destruction, palpable, crescendo, beacon, etching.*
* **Rule 2 (Zero Em Dashes):** Do not use em dashes (`—` or `--`) as mid-sentence crutches in prose. Use commas, periods, or semicolons. Break long, winding thoughts into tight, distinct sentences.
* **Rule 3 (Zero Superficial `-ing` Tags):** Eliminate dangling participial filler (*"showcasing his might, highlighting their fear, ensuring absolute silence"*). State the physical consequence with active, declarative verbs.
* **Rule 4 (No Cheap Puffery):** Replace abstract claims (*"a testament to his unfathomable heritage"*) with concrete sensory facts (*"the stone floor ground to flour beneath his heels"*).

## PLAYBOOKS

### Playbook A: Draft Elevation via the 6 Masterwork Patterns
When given any draft, execute these sequential transformation passes:
1. **Canon & Entity Guard:** Verify established figures (Luo Fan, Luo Clan); ensure zero unrequested names or backstories; use functional titles for extras.
2. **Pattern 1 Pass (Sensory Opening Anchor):** Anchor the opening in physical reality. Sentence 1 sets environment (light, sound, temperature), Sentence 2 shows a micro-action with a physical object, and Sentence 3 shows the room's atmospheric reaction.
3. **Pattern 2 Pass (Sovereign Dialogue Cadence):** Opponent formal speech -> Sovereign pause or micro-action -> Understated quiet strike -> Opponent physical reaction beat.
4. **Pattern 3 Pass (Asymmetric Psychological Leverage):** Reframe confrontations so the protagonist uses social leverage on third-party superiors, avoiding shouting matches.
5. **Pattern 4 Pass (Conceptual Weight over Energy Blasts):** Replace generic energy blasts with physical stoppage, concept overwriting (time, decay, stillness), and silent decisive slaughter.
6. **Pattern 5 Pass (Mobile Accordion Rhythm):** Enforce mobile cadence. Alternate between a 2-3 sentence atmospheric block, a 1-sentence punch, clean speech in `""`, a physical reaction, and a clean thought in `''`.
7. **Pattern 6 Pass (Chapter Cliffhanger Hook):** Ensure the chapter closes on an active hook (a sudden sound, an ominous decree, an unseen arrival).
8. **Unslop Cleanse:** Strip all AI buzzwords (delve, tapestry, testament, etc.), remove all em dashes, and enforce active verbs.

### Playbook B: Concept-to-Chapter Generation
When given raw bullet points or a scene idea, build the chapter using the 6 Masterwork Patterns:
1. **Author Intent & Vision Alignment:** Identify the core conflict and desired resolution without imposing arbitrary length caps.
2. **Pattern 1 Anchor:** Open with environmental lighting, acoustic details, and a tactile micro-action.
3. **Patterns 2 & 3 Escalation:** Build social tension through asymmetric leverage and sovereign dialogue cadence.
4. **Pattern 4 Climax:** Resolve the confrontation through conceptual law manipulation, system manifestation, or silent decisive eradication.
5. **Pattern 5 Flow:** Maintain the mobile accordion rhythm across all paragraphs.
6. **Pattern 6 Cliffhanger:** Snap shut on a sudden omen, arrival, or decree.
7. **Unslop Polish:** Verify zero em dashes, zero AI buzzwords, and strict punctuation (`""`, `''`, `[]`).

### Playbook C: Scene Staging & Pacing Direction
* Match the scene to the corresponding archetype in `04_engine/scene_archetypes.md`:
  - **Archetype 1 (The Sovereign Descent):** Environmental distortion -> Entourage heraldry -> Physical reveal -> Collective paralysis -> Effortless decree.
  - **Archetype 2 (The Court Chessboard):** False polite banquets -> Subtle provocation -> Sovereign amusement -> Razor-sharp expose -> Immediate consequence.
  - **Archetype 3 (Harvesting Children of Luck):** Target's peak arrogance -> Dissection of their secret -> Breakdown of morale -> Clean extraction -> Eradication of roots.
  - **Archetype 4 (Dao Comprehension & Mortal Contrast):** Solitary observation -> Mundane Dao metaphor -> Existential reflection -> Concept crystallization -> External resonance.
  - **Archetype 5 (System Manifestation):** Sovereign need -> Silent intent -> Genesis pulse -> Onlooker existential shock -> Seamless integration.
  - **Archetype 6 (The Social Squeeze & Tea-Table Humiliation):** Challenger provocation -> Sovereign disregard -> Third-party wedge command -> Immediate obedience -> Total humiliation.
  - **Archetype 7 (The Domino Cascade Climax):** Adversary's false triumph -> Missing variable revealed -> Trap snap -> Decisive reversal -> Heavy silence.
  - **Archetype 8 (The Decisive Root-Pulling Execution):** Formation of intent -> Zero monologue -> Complete physical and ancestral erasure -> Severing karma -> Clean departure.

### Playbook D: Cliffhanger & Retention Engineering
* Never end on passive completion.
* End on:
  1. An unexpected arrival or spatial tear.
  2. A chilling realization or hidden truth revealed.
  3. A cold decree or impending judgment.
  4. An unprecedented cosmological phenomenon.

### Playbook E: Continuous Canon & Outline Auto-Sync
* **Post-Chapter Automation:** Immediately after completing or refining any chapter, automatically synchronize both the world canon (`01_bible/`) and narrative outline (`02_outlines/`):
  1. **Bible Track (`01_bible/`):**
     * **Character Updates (`characters/`):** Record Luo Fan's realm breakthroughs, new techniques, or battle achievements. Update statuses of recurring figures.
     * **System Log (`system/`):** Record any new wishes granted by the Supreme Invincible System with chapter provenance.
     * **Faction & World Tracking (`factions/` & `settings/`):** Update diplomatic/war status of affected sects and log newly revealed locations/dimensional laws.
     * **Treasures & Artifacts (`treasures/`):** Document manifested primordial items, flames, or weapons.
  2. **Outline Track (`02_outlines/`):**
     * **Chapter Chronicle (`chapter_log.md`):** Append chapter synopsis, key actions, system wishes, cliffhanger, and word count.
     * **Master Storyline (`master_storyline.md`):** Update active arc milestones, mark resolved threads, and track upcoming planned confrontations.
  3. **Provenance Ledger:** Ensure all updates are non-destructive, appending clean chapter-tagged entries.

## FAILURE MODES & MITIGATIONS

| Failure Mode | Root Cause | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| White-Room Syndrome | Focusing only on dialogue/action | Force 2 sensory anchors (light, sound, texture) in opening lines. |
| Protagonist Pettiness | Treating invincible hero like an underdog | Strip all defensive explanations. Protagonist acts with calm certainty. |
| Cliché Reaction Spam | Lazy filler to show crowd shock | Ban "cold breaths". Use physical micro-tells (trembling fingers, silent retreats). |
| Rigid Formula Constraints | Forcing arbitrary act structures or length caps | Let the author's vision and scene pacing lead organically. |
| Info-Dump Fatigue | Explaining lore in encyclopedia chunks | Weave lore into active dialogue, immediate environmental consequences, or observations. |
| Punctuation Mismatch | Using quotes inconsistently or using asterisks | Enforce `""` for speech, `''` for thoughts, `[]` for system; strip all asterisks. |
| Clunky Scene Cuts | Using "Meanwhile...", breaks (`***`), or abrupt jumps | Apply Sensory Echo, Camera Pan, Cause-and-Effect Pivot, or Environmental Dissolve. |
| Canon Invariant Violation | Altering existing names or inventing new character names | Work strictly from author's prompt and bible; use functional titles for background figures. |
| Stale Canon or Outline | Failing to record new story developments or chapter logs | Execute Playbook E after every chapter; auto-update `01_bible/` and `02_outlines/`. |
| AI Slop and Buzzword Sludge | Defaulting to generic LLM prose tropes | Ban words (delve, tapestry, testament, palpable); ban em dashes (`—`); use declarative verbs. |

## VERIFICATION GATE

Before finalizing any rewritten draft or new chapter:
- [ ] Canon Invariant Gate: Zero altered or hallucinated character names; zero unsolicited character backstories.
- [ ] Unnamed background extras use functional titles only ("the elder", "the guard"), never invented proper names.
- [ ] Chapter faithfully realizes the author's draft/concept and natural creative vision.
- [ ] Protagonist tone matches canon: composed, regal, unhurried, razor-sharp.
- [ ] Scene transitions are 100% seamless (zero "Meanwhile...", zero `***`, zero jarring perspective whiplash).
- [ ] Punctuation strictly adheres: `""` for speech, `''` for thoughts, `[]` for system.
- [ ] Zero asterisks (`*` or `**`) in prose text or dialogue.
- [ ] Zero em dashes (`—` or `--`) in narrative text or dialogue.
- [ ] Zero presence of AI slop vocabulary ("delve", "testament", "tapestry", "intricate", "pivotal", "palpable", etc.).
- [ ] Zero presence of translatese or banned reaction clichés ("cold breath", etc.).
- [ ] Every scene grounded with lighting, acoustic, or tactile anchors.
- [ ] Chapter concludes with an active, forward-propelling cliffhanger.
- [ ] Continuous Canon & Outline Sync: `01_bible/` and `02_outlines/` (chapter_log.md, master_storyline.md) are automatically synchronized.
